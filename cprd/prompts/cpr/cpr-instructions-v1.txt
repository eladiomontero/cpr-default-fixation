Instructions:
You and other 3 participants will be asked to choose how many tokens to extract from a shared resource. 
The sum of all extractions of the group will result in a group payoff. 
This payoff is then distributed to participants proportional to how much they extracted from the
shared resource. This means that your earnings will depend on your choice
and the choices of the other 3 group members. Pick exactly one number between 0 and 30.
The more tokens you extract from the finite resource, the more we donate to a charity.
