Instructions:
You and the other 3 members of your group each start with 50 ECU in a
Private Account. Each of you will be asked to choose how many ECU to
contribute to a Public Account. You must choose exactly one value from
{0, 2, 4}.

If the group's total contribution (yours plus the other 3 members') is at
least 80 ECU, every group member keeps 100% of their Private Account. If
the group's total contribution is below 80 ECU, every group member keeps
only 10% of their Private Account (loses 90%).

This means your earnings depend on both your choice and the choices of the
other 3 group members.

Answer format: respond with a single number, one of 0, 2, or 4, and nothing
else.
