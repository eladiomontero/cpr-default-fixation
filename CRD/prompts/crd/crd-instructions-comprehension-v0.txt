Instructions:
You and the other 3 members of your group each start with 50 ECU in a
Private Account. Each round, every group member chooses how many ECU to
contribute to a shared Public Account. You must choose exactly one value
from {0, 2, 4} each round. This task lasts 10 rounds.

At the end of each round, if the group's total contribution (yours plus
the other 3 members') is at least 80 ECU, every group member keeps 100%
of their Private Account for that round. If the group's total
contribution is below 80 ECU, every group member keeps only 10% of their
Private Account for that round (loses 90%).

Contributions to the Public Account cannot be recovered.

This means your earnings depend on both your choice and the choices of
the other 3 group members.
